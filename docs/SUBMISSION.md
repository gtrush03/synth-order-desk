## What the submission demonstrates

A spoken merchandise request becomes a checked quote. Changing 150 shirts to 140 produces a $1,630 plan and clears any prior approval. After explicit approval, Synth prepares a work packet and launch kit, reads the supplier site through Tenki, saves a recipient-free Gmail draft, publishes the approved work ticket, verifies one unsubmitted Printful sample draft and sends the owner the approved demo email.

A new conversation remembers “never split shipments,” rejects the $1,800 request and accepts $1,950 express delivery when the budget rises to $2,000. The fresh run passed all 18 recorded steps. Hotdata performed two fresh waves with six scoped databases and six confirmed deletions; four RocketRide Python checks agreed and terminated. HydraDB supplies constraints, Rote replays two procedures, Cognee persists rules, and Snyk provides recorded security analysis.
